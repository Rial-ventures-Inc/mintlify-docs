<p className="rial-lede">
  RIAL tells you whether a photo is real. There are two ways to use it.
</p>

- **Capture**: someone takes the photo now. Send them a link that opens the camera in their browser, or put the capture inside your own app with the mobile SDKs.
- **Audit**: the photo already exists. Send the file and get the answer in seconds, with no one on the other end.

Both give you the same result: which checks tripped, where and when the photo was taken, and what it shows. You can run all of it from the API, the Node SDK, the CLI or the dashboard, or hand it to your AI agent over MCP.
