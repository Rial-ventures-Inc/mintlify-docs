---
title: "React Native SDK"
description: "Capture inside your React Native app."
---

A thin JS API over the native Kotlin and Swift SDKs: camera, attestation and offline queue stay native.

The SDK and its documentation are private. To get access, write to [developers@rial.io](mailto:developers@rial.io?subject=React%20Native%20SDK%20access) with your company and the app you're building.

Until then, the [REST API](/quickstart) works from any backend: same verdict, same capture link.
