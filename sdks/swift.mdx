---
title: "Swift SDK"
description: "Capture inside your iOS app."
---

The Swift SDK takes the photo inside your app, signs it with App Attest, depth and location, and submits it. Works offline; resumes if the app is killed.

The SDK and its documentation are private. To get access, write to [ulises@rial.io](mailto:ulises@rial.io?subject=Swift%20SDK%20access) with your company and the app you're building. You'll get the package, the integration guide and a publishable key.

Until then, the [REST API](/quickstart) works from any backend: same verdict, same capture link.
