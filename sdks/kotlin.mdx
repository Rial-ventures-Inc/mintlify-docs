---
title: "Kotlin SDK"
description: "Capture inside your Android app."
---

The Kotlin SDK takes the photo inside your app, signs it with device signals and submits it. Works offline; resumes if the app is killed.

The SDK and its documentation are private. To get access, write to [developers@rial.io](mailto:developers@rial.io?subject=Kotlin%20SDK%20access) with your company and the app you're building. You'll get the package, the integration guide and a publishable key.

Until then, the [REST API](/quickstart) works from any backend: same verdict, same capture link.
